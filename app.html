What it does

  • A score out of 100, not just a letter grade — so a shared result can actually be checked.
  • Ultra-processed food assessment, based on what the ingredient list says.
  • The additives that are really there, named by E-code, including the ones that cost nothing but are worth knowing.
  • Photograph a label when a product isn't in any database, and add it yourself.
  • Four languages: English, Türkçe, Polski, Español.

How the score works

Nutri-Score grades alone don't tell you much: an "A" and a "D" can sit on labels with near-identical sugar and salt. Secye scores the panel per 100 g out of 100, then reads the ingredient list for processing signals, so the number and the words on the label are considered together.

The model is versioned and its weights are published, so a score can be reproduced and argued with rather than taken on trust.

Privacy

A score is a calculation, not a health record, and Secye does not need your identity to do it. Photos you submit for a missing product are stripped of location and device metadata, deleted once the entry is complete, and kept for at most six months. You can delete your account and your data from inside the app.
