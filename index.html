Secye — Food Label Scanner

Secye is a mobile application for iOS and Android that scans food product barcodes and presents nutritional information, a Nutri-Score grade, an ultra-processed food (UPF) assessment, and a list of additives, so you can understand what is in a product while you are standing in the aisle.

Product data is drawn from Open Food Facts, a community-maintained open database, together with corrections submitted by our users and reviewed by us.

Secye is published by Adadeo Ltd. The documents below govern its use.

Health & Nutrition Disclaimer

Secye does not provide medical, dietary or health advice. It is an information and education tool only.

Allergies and intolerances

Never rely on Secye to decide whether a product is safe for an allergy or intolerance. Ingredient and allergen data may be incomplete, out of date, or wrong, and manufacturers change recipes without notice. Always read the physical label on the packaging, and follow the advice of your doctor or dietitian. If you have a severe allergy, treat the app’s information as unverified.

Scores and ratings

Nutri-Score grades, the ultra-processed food (UPF) assessment, additive risk levels and the energy-density notes shown in Secye are automated calculations derived from available nutritional data. They are:

  • informational and educational only;
  • not a diagnosis, treatment, prescription or dietary plan;
  • not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional;
  • not an assessment of whether a product is suitable for you, your medical conditions, medications, allergies or dietary requirements.

Nutri-Score is a front-of-pack labelling system developed by external public health bodies. Our implementation is our own interpretation and may differ from an official score shown on packaging.

Data accuracy

Product information comes largely from Open Food Facts, a community-maintained open database, supplemented by user submissions. We do not independently verify every entry. Where product data is missing or incomplete, scores may be unavailable or misleading.

If you have a medical condition

If you are pregnant, managing a condition such as diabetes, coeliac disease, kidney disease or hypertension, or following a clinical diet, consult your doctor or a registered dietitian before making changes based on anything shown in Secye. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you read in this app.

Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Secye account and all data associated with it at any time.

From within the app

  • Open Secye and go to the Settings tab.
  • Make sure you are signed in.
  • Under Danger Zone, tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

By email

If you cannot access the app, email info@adadeo.ie from the address registered to your account and ask us to delete it. We will complete the deletion within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

What is deleted

Your account record, email address, password hash, favourites, scan history and shopping list are all erased permanently and cannot be recovered.

What is retained

  • Non-identifying scan analytics — barcode, match result, device OS and app version. These are never linked to your account, so they cannot be traced back to you and are not deleted.
  • Product corrections you submitted — kept as part of the shared catalogue, with no link to your identity.
  • Your subscription — deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.
